Sticky Alarm privacy policy

Effective 23 October 2026

Sticky Alarm ("the app") is developed by Liam Brady (“I”, “me”). This page says what the app does with your information. The short version: it keeps your reminders on your phone and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app holds

The reminders you create: each one’s title, notes, date and time, alert type (notification or ringtone), repeat rule, which sound you chose, and its history (completed, snoozed, missed). The app also keeps two settings: whether reminders are paused (“this is not my device”) and your default sounds.

All of this is stored on your phone, in the app’s private storage, where other apps can’t read it. It is not stored on any server, because there isn’t one.

What the app does not do

  • It has no account or sign-in.
  • It does not use the internet. The app does not ask for network access, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
  • It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking, and it contains no code from advertising or analytics companies.
  • It does not read your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • It does not sell, share or give your information to anyone, because I never receive it.

Permissions, and why the app asks

PermissionWhy
NotificationsTo show a reminder when it is due.
Exact alarmsSo a reminder goes off at the exact minute you set, like an alarm clock.
Full-screen alertsSo a due reminder can appear over your lock screen.
Foreground service (media playback)To play the alarm sound while a reminder is waiting for you.
Run at startupTo set your reminders again after your phone restarts.
Keep the device awakeTo light the screen and sound the alarm when a reminder is due.

When you choose a custom sound, the system’s own sound picker is used; the app keeps only a reference to the sound you picked, never a copy.

Backups

Android’s automatic cloud backup is switched off for this app. If you use Backup and restore, the app saves your reminders to a file in the place you choose, with the system’s save dialog. That file contains your reminders’ text, so keep it as private as you would any personal file. The app never uploads it, and I never see it. Restoring reads a file you pick and replaces the reminders on that phone.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your reminders stay until you remove them. Deleting a reminder in the app removes it from the phone, and uninstalling the app removes everything it stored. Backup files you made yourself stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Google Play

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect information such as installs and crash reports under Google’s own privacy policy. The app does not add to that and I do not receive your personal information.

Children

Sticky Alarm is a general-purpose reminder app. It is not designed specifically for children, but it does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it collects nothing: your reminders stay on your phone. If you set the app up for a child, everything in this policy applies to the child’s phone in the same way.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes what it does with information, I will update this page and the date at the top before the change reaches you.
