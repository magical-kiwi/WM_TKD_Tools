Privacy Policy for TSD Tools

Last updated: 29 July 2026

TSD Tools ("the app") is developed by Liam Brady. This policy explains what data the app collects - which is none.

Data collection

TSD Tools does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app does not require an internet connection, does not include analytics or advertising software development kits (SDKs), and does not request access to any device permissions.

Data you enter

Contender names and scores that you enter into the app are held only in the app's memory while it is open, and are cleared automatically when the app is closed. Nothing you enter is saved to persistent storage, sent over a network, or accessible to anyone other than the device the app is running on.

Third parties

TSD Tools does not integrate with, or share data with, any third-party service, advertiser, or analytics provider.

Children's privacy

TSD Tools does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information from any user.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future - for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that does collect data - this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to 2guysinasheddev@gmail.com.
